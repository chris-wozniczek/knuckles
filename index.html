How to play

Five moves. Now cook.

Tap

Flick your fingertip onto a pad and stop, like a drumstick landing. A quick bounce repeats it.

Pinch

Touch thumb + index over a pad, or for an extra 808 / horn stab.

Raise & lower

Your right hand height picks the 808 note, roll speed or flute note.

Fist

In Hat Rolls, hold a fist to cut the beat, open it to drop.

Spread

Move hands apart for a bigger room.
